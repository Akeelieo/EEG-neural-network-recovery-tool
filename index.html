NHS
Neural Network Inference Tool

Brain connectivity from EEG

This tool recovers the directed connectivity network between brain regions from multichannel EEG recordings, and groups patients by the shape of that network. It is intended to support review of epileptic seizure recordings, not to replace clinical judgement.
